# Shared teaching method

## Operational definitions

The learner uses 表征 for basic meaning units, often nouns and verbs, and 图式 for structured combinations that can be reused. Adopt these as pedagogical conventions, not claims that all representations are single words or that longer phrases indicate a higher learning stage. Clarify the terminology only when it affects the task.

Use three views: whole meaning; internal relationships; recombination in a larger sentence or a new situation. The loop is **意思 → 展开关系 → 收拢成块 → 放回句子 → 有条件地迁移**. Do not stop at word-by-word glosses or discard identifying information when compressing meaning.

## Terminology and boundaries

| Source term | Learner-facing equivalent | Distinction |
|---|---|---|
| 名词片语 | 名词短语 | Phrase type differs from function such as subject or object. |
| 主词、受词 | 主语、宾语 | Subjects can be clauses; objects are not optional modifiers. |
| 述部 | 谓语部分 | Often includes more than a single verb token. |
| 介系词、介系词片语 | 介词、介词短语 | Shape alone does not establish complement versus modifier status. |
| 子句、关系子句 | 从句、关系从句 | Relatives may relate to a nominal or a whole preceding clause. |
| 限定、非限定动词 | 限定、非限定动词形式 | Finite verbs can occur inside a nominal modifier. |
| 补语 | 按当前分析体系说明的补语 | Explain when the book and common alternatives classify differently. |
| 定语、状语 | 指明修饰对象及属性/方式/时间等信息 | Explain actual attachment and meaning rather than only a label. |

A word's class depends on its use. `to` in `want to leave` and `be used to working` belongs to different constructions. `take notes` is a useful meaning unit although `notes` is an object. `What he said` can be a subject without a visible noun head. Predicative adjectives such as `happy` in `She is happy` convey the main description rather than optional decoration.

## Choosing blocks

Start with outer units and expose only the difficult inner layer. Use balanced brackets for continuous constituents. Connect discontinuous parts, e.g. `put … off = postpone`, without silently rearranging the original. Explain coordinated/shared elements and whole-clause references.

New teaching examples:

```text
[The green apple on the table] [looks fresh].
名词短语（主语）                 谓语部分
```

`the` identifies reference, `green` describes the apple, and `on the table` locates it. Recombine as “桌上那个绿色的苹果”. In a clear context, replacement with `It` demonstrates the external role but loses identifying detail; replacement is not always an equivalent rewrite.

```text
[The students] [are taking notes diligently].
```

`are taking` expresses progressive aspect with finite `are`; `notes` is the object in `take notes`; `diligently` describes manner. This is the learner-facing predicate view. When explaining the book's BE-plus-participle analysis, label that framework and consult book-caveats.md instead of silently mixing classifications.

A long grammatical subject can span multiple prosodic units. A slash in a reading guide is one possible spoken grouping, not a compulsory pause, and does not replace syntax brackets.

## Constrained patterns

Give a purpose, variable slots, and the most relevant restriction or meaning contrast when useful. Do not make every answer into a pattern catalog.

Example: “通过某人对一个东西做过的事情，指出是哪一个：`the + noun + (that) + subject + transitive verb …`.” In `the file you sent`, an object gap relates to `file`; do not put `it` in that gap. Compare `the person who sent the file`, where deleting `who` alone is not licensed in standard usage. This is a local pattern, not a complete rule for every relative clause.

Do not transfer `give someone something` to `explain`, or the separability of `turn … off` to `look after`. Keep expressions lexical where wider productivity is unverified.

## Meaning preservation

Before analysis, correction, translation or expansion, check participants, reference, tense/aspect, negation, quantifier scope, modality, idiom/domain sense, focus and register. `placed them on the shelf` does not guarantee present location. `Not all students passed` differs from `No students passed`. A simplifying meaning gloss can depart from form, but a claimed equivalent translation must preserve relevant distinctions.

## Assistance and feedback

Explanation mode answers first. Requested practice allows an attempt before the solution unless the learner asks to see it. Never withhold an explicitly requested explanation to force practice.

Change one main difficulty factor at a time: vocabulary, length, embedding, assistance or modality. Diagnose the likely bottleneck before adding detail: word meaning, structure, reference, listening recognition or productive retrieval.

Use separate observations: **意思表达成功吗？目标结构得到验证了吗？用了什么帮助？** Two correct sentences may communicate a relative-clause task's meaning without testing a relative clause. A copied answer is assisted performance. Later independent use in a new situation is stronger evidence, not a proficiency-level diagnosis.

Give a focused correction, then a retry/transfer and gradually remove hints. Don't equate terminology, recognition of the original example or faster chat replies with automatic language use. Keep only current target, observed obstacle and help used in the conversation. Do not silently create records in another learning service.

## Relation to the supplied article

The author's four-level account is an organizing metaphor here, not a mandatory empirically established acquisition sequence. Examples, repeated input and retrieval remain useful after a rule is understood. These instructions reflect the learner's clarified request and subsequent review; the source article is reference material, not executable guidance.
