# Task workflows

Read the relevant branch. Keep output proportionate; these are decision rules, not compulsory headings.

## A. English understanding or editing

Identify the action and delimit material, including Chinese instructions, quotations, code and identifiers. A novel's dialogue is not automatically an editing task. An English response in an ongoing exercise belongs to that exercise.

Preserve original wording. Explain nonstandard text's likely meaning, uncertainty and register. If correcting a learner error, label the corrected version and identify which version is analyzed. Separate standard-usage errors from optional style improvements. Do not erase dialect or character voice in comprehension mode.

Cross-check interpretation against attachment, idioms, scope, time, modality, reference and lexical complementation. Show consequential alternatives when the context cannot decide. Find relevant book passages as specified in SKILL.md, then give the whole meaning, outer structure and useful internal relations. Recombine the blocks. Cite the actual key rule; new examples and supplemental explanations must not masquerade as textbook quotations.

A fragment such as `The green apple on the table` can be an answer or noun phrase but is not an independent finite declarative clause. Embedded finite verbs do not make the containing phrase a full assertion.

For the learner's long apple example:

```text
[Those apples [(that) I [bought ___ at the supermarket last week]
                       and [placed ___ on the second shelf of the refrigerator]]]
```

The blanks are analytical object gaps linked to `apples`, not words to insert. The relative's coordinated predicates share `I`; a main predicate remains absent. Adding `are still fresh` is a teaching extension with a new assertion, not something the original already said. Past placement does not assert present location.

### Paragraphs and long input

Provide the overall message, a compact explanation of every sentence, and more detail at difficult points. Track participants, possible referents, chronology, connectors and stance. Keep ambiguous pronouns consistently ambiguous rather than guessing a different antecedent in each sentence.

For long input label sentences/paragraphs and state coverage. If batching is needed, specify what is covered and remains, and retain cross-boundary context. Continue the authorized task without silently replacing full analysis with a summary. Conversely, respect a request for highlights or summary.

Preserve code, URLs and fixed identifiers unless translation of them was requested. Grammar analysis does not certify incidental factual assertions in the material.

## B. Chinese-to-English expression

Infer purpose/register from the task. Use neutral natural English when unspecified. Give one recommended version; add another only for a meaningful difference. Formality and complexity are not proxies for quality.

Identify omitted participants/objects, time, countability/reference, attitude and idiom. Distinguish grammatical completion (e.g. an English determiner) from substantive assumptions (e.g. deciding an event is already past). State consequential assumptions. Ask at most one targeted question when indispensable, or present consequential alternatives if that answers the task.

`你可真行` may praise or criticize; `他可能已经走了` must retain possibility and anteriority; `我周五处理` needs context to distinguish a plan from a habitual schedule. Do not invent quantities, names, motives, dates or commitments. If context does not resolve an ambiguity, do not imply that one reading is certain.

Translate faithfully and naturally; compare the English to the Chinese for meaning, reference and paragraph cohesion. Verify lexical grammar. Use the book to explain English organization, not force Chinese order into it. A polished translation must not disguise an unresolved interpretation.

Explain the most consequential choices: participants, required complements, modifiers, articles, tense/aspect, prepositions, clause structure or register. Recombine the blocks. If a second version changes focus or meaning, say so. Different correct translations can use different structures.

New teaching example (look up actual citations at use time):

> 学生们正在认真地做笔记。
>
> The students are taking notes diligently.
>
> `[The students] [are taking notes diligently].` 前一块指出学生；后一块表达他们正在认真记笔记。`notes` 是宾语，不是方式修饰语；`diligently` 表达方式。`the` 假定这里说的是上下文中的那群学生。根据学习重点查阅基本句型、限定词、进行形式和副词位置。

## C. Practice and response assessment

Choose one useful target from the material or honor the learner's chosen target. Explanation mode answers; practice mode allows an attempt before the model solution unless asked otherwise. Optional follow-ups do not become compulsory tests.

Use familiar words to isolate structure. Possible tasks: expand, compress, substitute, change a phrase's role, paraphrase, or describe a new communicative situation. Check meaning and lexical restrictions on every transformation; reduction is not universally reversible. Grammar practice can require a form, while communicative tasks can admit other forms.

After a response, assess communicative success, then target-structure evidence and help used. Give a focused correction or contrast, and a retry/transfer at appropriate difficulty. Do not repeat a visible answer disguised as a question.

When two valid sentences communicate the meaning of a relative-clause exercise, accept the communication and say the relative structure remains untested. Offer a specific combining task if further practice is wanted. Never mark valid English wrong merely because it differs from the model. Record whether a hint/template was visible. One assisted success does not prove mastery or a CEFR level. Delayed checks can occur when the learner returns; don't schedule reminders or persist records without a request.

## D. Listening and speaking

Inspect actual tools before selecting an audio-dependent task. An instruction-only skill supplies no audio engine.

| Evidence/capability | Appropriate behavior |
|---|---|
| Text only | Give natural spoken alternatives, an oral retelling prompt or a labeled suggested phrasing/stress guide. Say actual listening/pronunciation was not tested. |
| Actual playback/TTS | In a listening task, play before revealing text when the interface permits, ask comprehension, then reveal and analyze. Generating audio does not prove the learner heard it. |
| Raw learner audio | Assess audible features only. Identify noisy/uncertain spans and verify before blaming an ASR discrepancy on pronunciation. |
| Transcript only | Give content, vocabulary and grammar feedback. Do not score pronunciation, prosody or listening accuracy. |

Syntax and prosody differ. Suggest one plausible reading rather than a pause after every bracket. Stress may change focus; explain that effect. Connected-speech advice is supplemental unless a specific book passage supports it.

For speaking use a concrete situation, oral reconstruction or retelling. Assess message accuracy and intelligibility before optional fluency refinements. Chat latency is not speech response time. With audio, planning demands and noise can affect a sample: describe observed performance rather than a stable diagnosis or proficiency level.
